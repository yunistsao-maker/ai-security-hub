Because the AI Basic Act has no penalties, enforcement for an AI security incident runs through these laws and through sector licences.

## Cyber Security Management Act (資通安全管理法)

The amendments passed third reading on 29 August 2025, were promulgated on 24 September 2025 and took effect on 1 December 2025.

| Point | What changed |
|---|---|
| Authority | The competent authority moved from the Executive Yuan to the Ministry of Digital Affairs |
| Who is covered | Government agencies, specified non-government agencies and critical infrastructure providers |
| Banned products | Agencies may not use products that endanger national cyber security, and the authority may restrict specified non-government agencies likewise. MODA issued an interpretation of the term on 29 June 2026 |
| People | Specified non-government agencies appoint dedicated security staff and a chief information security officer |
| Suppliers | Outsourcing needs a written contract and participation in drills |
| Fines | Up to NT$10 million for failing to report a cyber security incident |

## Personal Data Protection Act (個人資料保護法)

The amendments were promulgated on 11 November 2025, with the commencement date to be set by the Executive Yuan. Draft rules published on 22 January 2026 cover data protection officers, the security of personal data files, and incident handling, including **notifying data subjects within 72 hours** of learning of an incident. On 7 October 2026 the authority still operated as the Preparatory Office of the Personal Data Protection Commission; check commencement before relying on these duties.

## What this means for an AI system

- If an AI application processes personal data, a leak through prompt injection or an over-privileged agent is a personal data incident under the PDPA.
- If the operator is a covered agency or critical infrastructure provider, the Cyber Security Management Act's reporting duty and fines apply to incidents involving its AI systems.
- Supplier duties reach AI vendors: written contracts, drills and, in finance, SBOMs.
