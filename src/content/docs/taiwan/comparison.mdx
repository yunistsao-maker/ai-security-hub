Taiwan's Act is closest to Japan's: a framework law without penalties that leaves binding detail to later rules. The EU AI Act is the opposite model, with direct duties and fines. South Korea sits between them, and China regulates through many specific measures instead of one AI law.

## Incident-reporting clocks

Reporting deadlines are the most concrete security duty across jurisdictions.

| Deadline | Where it applies |
|---|---|
| 4 hours, preliminary report | China, relatively major incidents |
| 24 hours, then 72 hours | EU Cyber Resilience Act |
| 72 hours | New York RAISE Act, from 1 January 2027 |
| 72 hours to data subjects | Taiwan, draft personal data rules |
| 15 days, or 24 hours for imminent risk | California SB 53 |
