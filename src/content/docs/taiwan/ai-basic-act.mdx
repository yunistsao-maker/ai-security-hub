The Artificial Intelligence Basic Act (人工智慧基本法) is a framework law addressed to government. It sets principles and assigns tasks, has no penalties, and leaves binding rules for companies to sector regulators, who have until January 2028 to write them.

## Key facts

| Item | What the Act says |
|---|---|
| In force | Promulgated and in effect on 14 January 2026 |
| Competent authority | The National Science and Technology Council (國科會) centrally; city and county governments locally (Article 2) |
| Definition of AI | A system capable of autonomous operation that, through input or sensing and by machine learning and algorithms, generates outputs such as predictions, content, recommendations or decisions (Article 3) |
| Strategy body | A National AI Strategy Special Committee convened by the Premier, meeting at least once a year (Article 6) |
| Risk framework | The Ministry of Digital Affairs promotes a risk classification framework that interoperates with international ones (Article 16) |
| High-risk uses | Warning labels; the government clarifies liability and sets up remedy, compensation or insurance mechanisms (Articles 5 and 17) |
| Law review | Laws to be enacted, amended or repealed within two years (Article 18) |
| Government use | Agencies run risk assessments and set internal controls for their own AI use (Article 19) |
| Penalties | None |

## The seven principles

Article 4 sets seven principles. The fourth is the security principle.

1. Sustainable development and well-being (永續發展與福祉)
2. Human autonomy (人類自主)
3. Privacy protection and data governance (隱私保護與資料治理)
4. **Cybersecurity and safety (資安與安全):** cybersecurity measures shall be established to prevent security threats and attacks, ensuring the robustness and safety of the system
5. Transparency and explainability (透明與可解釋)
6. Fairness and non-discrimination (公平與不歧視)
7. Accountability (問責)

## Timetable

The NSTC implementation plan sets these deadlines, counted from 14 January 2026:

| By | What is due |
|---|---|
| About 14 July 2026 | Government agencies using AI complete risk assessments (6 months) |
| About 14 January 2027 | Agencies adopt internal control rules (1 year) |
| About 14 January 2028 | Sector regulators set risk-management rules; the review of existing laws is complete (2 years) |

## What it means for companies

The Act places no direct duties on companies. For now, binding security obligations come from the [Cyber Security Management Act, the Personal Data Protection Act](/taiwan/cyber-and-data-laws/) and sector supervision such as the [Financial Supervisory Commission's](/taiwan/risk-framework/). Expect sector rules written in the vocabulary of MODA's risk framework before January 2028, with labour, finance, health and education first.
