Every principle set treats security as one named dimension of responsible AI, information security is the most common owner of AI governance, and frontier safety frameworks are where developers now publish their weight-security commitments.

This site uses one working boundary. Responsible AI is the full set of properties. AI safety is about preventing harm from what a system does or enables. AI security is about protecting AI systems, data and model weights from adversaries, and protecting other systems from AI-enabled attack.
