The published consensus is architectural. Once an agent has read untrusted content, controls outside the model must bound what it can do, and filters are one layer among several.

Three rules carry most of the weight:

- **Authorise outside the model.** Model output is untrusted, however capable the model.
- **Isolate with the operating system or hypervisor,** not with instructions to the agent.
- **Limit the combination.** Without supervision, a session should combine at most two of untrusted input, access to sensitive data, and the ability to act externally.
