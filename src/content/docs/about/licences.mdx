## Reusing others' work

- Every page is written in our own words and links to its sources.
- Short quotations are credited.
- Framework identifiers and list titles, such as LLM01:2026 Prompt Injection, are used as references with credit. OWASP publishes its LLM Top 10 under CC BY-SA 4.0.
- Nothing is copied from ISO standards or from sources under non-commercial licences.
- News items are headlines with links; article text is not stored.
