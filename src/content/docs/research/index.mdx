The field spans 31 areas in six clusters. For individual studies, see [papers](/research/papers/) and [community write-ups and talks](/research/community/). Since 2024 its centre has moved from attacks on single models to the security of agent systems, and to AI systems that act as cyber operators.

## Recognised open problems

Each problem below is described as unsolved by the body or paper cited. They group under three causes: models enforce no boundary between instructions and data; what a model has learned cannot be verified from outside; and capability spreads faster than controls.
